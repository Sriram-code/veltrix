## 1. When this applies

Some roles, such as on-call engineering, may need to work on a public holiday. Work on a public holiday must be approved in advance by the manager.

## 2. Compensatory time off

For each full public holiday worked, the employee receives 1 day of compensatory time off. It must be used within 60 days of the holiday worked, or it is lost. Compensatory time off is recorded by the manager in the HR system and is separate from annual leave.

## 3. Weekends

Approved work on a weekend is compensated in the same way as work on a public holiday.
