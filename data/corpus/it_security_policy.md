## 1. Accounts and passwords

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for every company system.
- Never share your password, including with IT staff.

## 2. Devices

Only company-managed laptops may be used to access company systems. Personal phones may be used for email and chat only if the company mobile management app is installed.

## 3. Data handling

| Classification | Examples | Where it may be stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Marketing pages, published blog posts | Anywhere |
| Internal | Policies, team plans | Company drive and approved tools |
| Confidential | Customer data, salaries, performance records | Approved systems with restricted access only |

## 4. Reporting incidents

Report a lost device, suspicious email or possible data leak to security@veltrix.example within one hour of noticing it.

## 5. AI tools

Only AI tools approved by IT may be used with internal or confidential information. Never paste customer data or employee personal data into a public AI tool.
