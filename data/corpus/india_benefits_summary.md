## 1. Group health insurance

Employees, their spouse, up to two children and both parents are covered by group health insurance with a sum insured of ₹7,00,000 per family per year. Parents-in-law may be added at the employee's cost.

## 2. Provident fund and gratuity

Veltrix contributes 12% of basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund. Gratuity is paid as required by the Payment of Gratuity Act after five years of continuous service.

## 3. Other benefits

- Term life insurance of three times annual fixed pay.
- Meal card of ₹2,200 per month.
- Annual health check-up at partner hospitals.
