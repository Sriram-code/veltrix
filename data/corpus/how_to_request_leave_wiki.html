A practical guide written by employees. For the official rules, see the Global Leave Policy.

Steps

  1. Open the HR system and choose Leave, then New request.
  2. Pick the leave type and dates. The system shows how many working days will be used.
  3. Add a note for your manager if the request is short notice.
  4. Your manager gets an email and usually replies within a couple of days.

Tips

  • You can carry over 10 days, so plan your December breaks.
  • Sick leave is 10 days a year, so don't save it up for holidays.
  • If your request overlaps with a public holiday, the holiday is not counted.
