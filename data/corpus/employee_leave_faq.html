Answers to the questions we hear most often about leave. Last reviewed in May 2026.

How many sick days do I get?

You get 10 paid sick days every year. They are credited on 1 January.

When do I need a doctor's note?

If you are off sick for more than 2 working days in a row, please upload a medical certificate.

How much annual leave can I carry over?

You can carry over up to 10 days into next year. Use them by 30 June or you lose them.

How long is parental leave?

Primary caregivers get 16 weeks of paid leave and secondary caregivers get 2 weeks.

How many days do I get for bereavement?

Up to 3 working days for an immediate family member.

How many days a week can I work from home?

Up to 2 days a week, agreed with your manager.

Do public holidays count against my leave?

No. Weekends and public holidays inside your leave are not deducted.

Can I take half days?

No. All leave is taken in whole days.

Who do I contact with other questions?

Email hr@veltrix.example.
