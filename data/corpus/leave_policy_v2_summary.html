This page summarises the Global Leave Policy (Version 2). The full policy is the reference if anything on this page is unclear.

Leave at a glance

Leave typeAllowanceNotes
Annual leaveEarned monthly, see the Annual Leave Accrual ScheduleUp to 10 days can be carried forward; use them by 31 March
Sick leave12 working days per yearMedical certificate needed after 3 consecutive working days
Parental leave, primary caregiver20 weeksRequest 8 weeks before it starts
Parental leave, secondary caregiver4 weeksTake within 12 months, in up to two blocks
Bereavement leave5 working daysFor immediate family members
Unpaid leaveUp to 20 working days per yearAt the line manager's discretion

Requesting leave

  • Request all leave in the HR system before you take it, except unplanned sick leave.
  • For five or more working days of annual leave, ask at least two weeks ahead.
  • Weekends and public holidays inside your leave are not deducted from your balance.

Regional addenda may change some of these rules for your office. Check the addendum for India, the United States or Germany.
