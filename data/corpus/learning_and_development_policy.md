## 1. Learning budget

| Office | Annual learning budget per employee |
| --- | --- |
| Bengaluru, India | ₹60,000 |
| Austin, United States | $1,000 |
| Berlin, Germany | €900 |

The budget can be used for courses, certifications, conferences and books related to the employee's role. Unused budget does not carry forward.

## 2. Learning days

Employees may use up to 3 working days per year for approved learning activities. Learning days are agreed with the manager and recorded in the learning platform. They are not a type of leave and are not deducted from leave balances.

## 3. Repayment

If the company pays more than the equivalent of 2,000 US dollars for a single certification and the employee leaves within 12 months, 50% of the cost must be repaid.
